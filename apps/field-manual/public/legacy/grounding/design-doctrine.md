# Design Doctrine

## Product character

- Quiet operational core.
- Visible process.
- Traceable reasoning.
- Controlled visual identity.
- Expert density without clutter.
- Familiar controls unless method requirements justify novelty.

## Visual ancestry

Use these traditions as principles rather than costumes:

- Swiss information design: grid, alignment, hierarchy, typographic rigor.
- Industrial HMI: situational awareness, neutral normal states, semantic alerts.
- Transit systems: persistent orientation, ordered stations, clear gates.
- Scientific notebooks and logs: sequential reasoning, provenance, reproducibility.
- Modern accessible primitives: strong keyboard, touch, focus, and screen-reader behavior.

## Composition

- Prefer structural planes, lists, tables, routes, and readable documents over card soup.
- Keep decision-relevant context visible.
- Use asymmetric planes based on information importance.
- Preserve recurring element positions to build spatial memory.
- Mobile is a re-prioritized composition, not a shrunken desktop.
- Use whitespace to group information, not to create empty spectacle.

## Typography

- Sans serif for interface content and critical values.
- Monospace for IDs, dates, formulas, provenance, and value metadata.
- Optional editorial face for reflective questions, arguments, or dossier narratives.
- Use tabular numerals wherever values are compared.
- Avoid tiny metadata; test projected and zoomed use.

## Color

- Normal states remain neutral.
- Color encodes a named semantic role.
- Never rely on color alone.
- Reserve strong colors for focus, track identity, uncertainty, settlement, and blocking.
- Every state must survive grayscale and projector washout.

## Geometry

Case Systems may use modest rounding and depth.

Adoption Lane may override this with sharper geometry, fixed route symbols, and stricter angle rules.

## Density

Density is justified when it reduces navigation, recall burden, or hidden context.

Do not expose every administrative detail at all times. Keep decision context visible and allow secondary history or metadata to collapse.
